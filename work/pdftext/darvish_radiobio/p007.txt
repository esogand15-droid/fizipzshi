• Stochastic Effects
– Severity independent of dose (both somatic and genetic)
– Examples: leukemia, malignant tumors
• Non-stochastic Effects
– Severity varies with magnitude of dose, above a threshold dose (somatic)
– Examples: cataracts, fertility impairment
