Measuring Tissue Magnetization:
When Mo is aligned to the magnetic field Bo it is very difficult
to measure since Bo itself is millions of times stronger than Mo.
Mo may be measured by “tipping” it perpendicular to Bo.

===== page 18 =====
Measuring Tissue Magnetization:
Mo is “tipped” into the transverse plane by a burst of
electromagnetic radiation, commonly called an RF Pulse.
